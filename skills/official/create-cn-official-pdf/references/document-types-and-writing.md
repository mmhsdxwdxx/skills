## 1. Fifteen official-document types

Use the purpose defined by《党政机关公文处理工作条例》; do not choose a type merely because a local template looks similar.

| Type | Use |
|---|---|
| 决议 | Major decisions adopted through a meeting discussion. |
| 决定 | Decisions/arrangements on major matters; rewards or sanctions; changing or revoking inappropriate lower-level decisions. |
| 命令（令） | Promulgating administrative regulations/rules, announcing major compulsory measures, approving rank awards, rewards, or sanctions under authority. |
| 公报 | Announcing important decisions or major matters. |
| 公告 | Publicly announcing important or statutory matters domestically or abroad. |
| 通告 | Announcing matters that must be observed or known within a defined scope. |
| 意见 | Expressing views and handling measures on important issues. |
| 通知 | Issuing/forwarding documents, conveying matters to lower bodies, or communicating matters requiring action or knowledge. |
| 通报 | Commending, criticizing, conveying important spirit, or communicating important situations. |
| 报告 | Reporting work/situations to a superior or replying to an inquiry. Do not embed a request for approval. |
| 请示 | Requesting instruction or approval from a superior. Normally one document, one matter, one principal recipient. |
| 批复 | Replying to a subordinate's request for instruction. |
| 议案 | A government submits a matter for deliberation to the people's congress or its standing committee under statutory procedure. |
| 函 | Negotiating, asking/answering, requesting approval from, or replying to an authority without a subordinate relationship. |
| 纪要 | Recording the principal situation and agreed decisions of a meeting. |

`信函格式` is a layout, while `函` is a document type. A 通知, 批复, or other type may use letter layout if the competent office authorizes it.

## 2. Direction and routing controls

- Determine the relationship and authority before drafting: upward, downward, parallel/non-subordinate, public proclamation, or meeting record.
- Use upward form and signer names for upward documents.
- Normally submit upward documents to one principal recipient; copy other relevant superiors rather than multi-addressing a request.
- A request should contain one matter and must not be embedded in a report.
- Do not ordinarily send a request simultaneously to a subordinate.
- Do not directly address an official personally unless the rules and circumstances allow it.
- Do not issue commands beyond the issuing authority's statutory or assigned powers.

## 3. Drafting controls

The regulation requires drafts to comply with law and policy; accurately reflect the issuing authority's intent; proceed from facts; use feasible measures; be concise, focused, clear, rigorous, accurate, and economical; use the correct document type and format; conduct necessary research and consultation; coordinate matters within another authority's powers; and state people, places, dates, numbers, paragraph order, and citations accurately.

Do not fabricate policy bases, approval status, consultations, meeting decisions, issuing authority, signer, or effective date.

## 4. Language and notation controls

- Follow current national standards for Chinese characters, punctuation, numbers, measurement units, and foreign characters.
- Prefer full-width Chinese punctuation in Chinese prose: `，。；：？！“”‘’（）《》〔〕`.
- Use hexagonal brackets `〔〕` where required, notably document numbers; do not substitute `[]`, `【】`, or parentheses.
- Follow GB/T 15835 for Arabic versus Chinese numerals. Do not impose “all numbers must be Arabic” without context.
- Use SI/legal units and keep number-unit groups together.
- Verify official names and cited-document titles. Put titled laws/rules/documents in book-title marks where grammatically appropriate.
- A standalone heading normally has no terminal punctuation.
- A title normally omits punctuation other than necessary book-title marks.

## 5. Content preflight

- [ ] The issuing authority has jurisdiction.
- [ ] The document type matches the intended legal/administrative act.
- [ ] Direction, recipient, copy recipients, and signer requirements agree.
- [ ] A request contains one matter and requests an explicit action.
- [ ] A report contains no hidden request.
- [ ] Policy and legal bases are current and quoted accurately.
- [ ] Names, dates, numbers, citations, annexes, and terminology are consistent.
- [ ] Attachments are referenced, present, and named identically.
- [ ] Effective date, validity, repeal, interpretation, and disclosure clauses are included when required.
- [ ] Confidentiality, urgency, and circulation labels have actual authorization.
- [ ] Final text has completed the issuing authority's required review, consultation, approval, and signature process.
